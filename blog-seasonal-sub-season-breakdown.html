Understanding the 12-Season Framework: Deep Sub-Seasons Explored

Traditional four-season color analysis was a great starting point, but grouping everyone into just Spring, Summer, Autumn, or Winter can overlook crucial details. The modern 12-season system builds on this by adding three distinct sub-categories to each season, offering a much more precise look at your unique color profile.

The Core Properties: Hue, Value, and Chroma

Sub-seasons look closely at the fine details of your features—evaluating your warmth vs. coolness (hue), your overall lightness vs. darkness (value), and how muted or vibrant your natural contrast is (chroma).

A Closer Look at the Sub-Seasons

This approach allows for helpful distinctions. For instance, a Soft Summer thrives in muted, dusty, cool tones, while a Bright Winter requires high-contrast, vibrant, striking shades. Pinpointing your exact sub-season makes it easy to find the colors that truly complement your face.
