Behind the Pixels: How Client-Side Computer Vision Works

Many online tools ask you to upload personal photographs to a remote server, raising natural privacy concerns. Our platform takes a different, privacy-first approach. By utilizing client-side web frameworks, your images are analyzed entirely on your own device, ensuring your data never leaves your browser.

Locating Facial Regions with face-api.js

Our script utilizes local machine learning models via face-api.js to isolate key facial regions—like your cheeks and forehead—while filtering out distracting background elements or hair shadows that could skew the analysis.

The Science of Pixel Sampling

Once the facial region is mapped, the application runs a local K-means clustering algorithm to determine your true dominant skin pigments. By evaluating these values across the YCbCr spectrum, it calculates your precise seasonal color profile entirely in temporary browser memory.
